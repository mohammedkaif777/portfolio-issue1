## Core Technical Directives

### 1. Dual-Mode Comic Design System ("Multiverse Shift")
- **Mode 1 - Cyber Neon (Spider-Verse):** Vibrant Magenta (`#FF007F`), Electric Cyan (`#00F0FF`), Acid Yellow (`#FFE600`), Dark Ink (`#0D0D15`).
- **Mode 2 - Comic Noir (Gotham / Dark):** Gotham Slate (`#16161E`), Amber Gold (`#F59E0B`), Chalk White (`#F3F4F6`), Deep Shadow Black (`#08080C`).
- **Inking & Shadows:** Solid 3px–4px `#111111` ink borders and neobrutalist offset drop shadows (`box-shadow: 5px 5px 0px [accent]`).
- **Halftone Patterns:** SVG-based halftone dot matrices dynamically adjusted per theme.
- **Action Bursts:** Render dynamic starburst stickers ("POW!", "WHAM!", "DEBUGGED!", "LLM BOOST!").
- **Typography:** Google Fonts `Bangers` (Headlines), `Comic Neue` (Body), `Press Start 2P` (Game HUD).

### 2. Side-Scrolling Runner Mini-Game ("Cyber-Runner: Lords of the Codes")
- **Canvas Engine:** Pure 2D HTML5 canvas loop at 60fps with delta-time.
- **Controls:** Spacebar, Up Arrow, or Touch tap for Jump / Double-Jump.
- **Audio Synthesizer:** Zero-asset procedural Web Audio API sounds (jumps, pickups, power-ups, crash). Default state: Sound ON with immediate navbar mute toggle.
- **Scoring & Rewards:** Score tracking with `localStorage` high score persistence. Scoring 300+ points unlocks the Gold Comic Badge and secret resume dossier.
